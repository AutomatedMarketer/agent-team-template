# Content — workspace

`output/` holds drafts, one dated file per run. Nothing here is published. You read it,
edit it, and post it yourself.

`notes/` is your drop folder. Voice-note transcripts, half-thoughts, links, anything you
want turned into content — throw it in here in any shape. The draft queue reads it.

## First workflow

This agent arrives owning **Draft Queue** (`workflows/draft-queue.yml`) — every weekday,
whatever landed in `notes/` becomes post drafts in your voice.

## Operating rules

- **Your words are the raw material.** Ideas are quoted from your notes, not paraphrased
  away — the phrasing in a voice note *is* the voice.
- **Facts come from the business brain.** A claim not backed by
  `shared/business-brain.md` — including its verified claims register — gets softened or
  cut before it reaches a draft.
- **No voice samples, no invented personality.** With `shared/writing-rules.md` unfilled
  it writes plainly and says so, rather than guessing at how you sound.
- **Drafts only.** Every queue ends with "Nothing was published", and it is true on
  every run.
