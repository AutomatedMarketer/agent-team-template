# FAQ

The customer service agent answers only from this file. If a question is not here, it says
so rather than guessing, and the question is logged so you can add it.

**If the customers are not yours, say so here and stop.** Replace the first marker with
something like *"I do not deal with customers - I work for this business and support is
<name>'s"*, delete the rest of the file's markers, and you are done. That is a finished FAQ, not
an empty one: `/audit` reads it as **Not in use** rather than a gap. This is common for anyone
with a job rather than a company, and the course says so on Day 1.

**Then consider what you would put here instead.** The discipline this file teaches - *answer
only from the written source, say "I don't know" rather than infer, log the gap* - is the right
shape for any answer bank whose wrong answers are expensive. A prequalification questionnaire, a
compliance response set, a policy desk. Same file, different questions.
